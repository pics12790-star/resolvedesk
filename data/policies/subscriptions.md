# SUB-01 | Subscriptions, renewals, and cancellation requests

Effective 2026-01-01. Version 1.

Subscription policy applies to recurring services, support plans, protection plans, and membership renewals. A product replacement policy does not cancel a subscription and a subscription cancellation does not refund a physical product order unless another policy authorizes it.

If a customer asks to cancel future renewal, verify account ownership and cancel renewal if the system supports it. If the customer asks for refund of a renewal already charged, check the renewal refund window or escalate when no automatic rule exists.

If a customer claims they did not know about renewal, do not argue. Explain the recorded renewal state and escalate if the charge is disputed.

Subscription changes should not expose internal plan margins, retention scripts, or churn-risk scoring. The agent can provide customer-visible plan name, renewal date, and cancellation status.

If the customer has both a hardware order and support subscription, the agent should keep the issues separate in the ticket summary.
