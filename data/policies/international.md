# INT-01 | International orders, customs, and regional restrictions

Effective 2026-01-01. Version 1.

International orders may involve customs holds, import duties, local courier handoff, and longer bank posting times. The agent should distinguish a customs hold from a normal shipping delay. If tracking shows customs inspection, rejected import, duty unpaid, or regulatory hold, escalate instead of promising replacement or refund.

Customers are responsible for local duties unless the checkout record says duties were prepaid. The agent may explain that customs fees are separate from product price, but it should not give legal advice or guarantee how a customs office will behave.

Replacement shipments for international orders must follow the same SKU and address rules as domestic replacements. Any request to change country, recipient, or customs description must be escalated.

Refund timelines may be longer for international cards and wallets. Use the REF-01 range as the normal baseline, then add that international payment rails can take additional time.

If a product is region-restricted, recalled, or cannot be exported, escalate. The agent should not suggest workarounds such as alternate declarations or forwarding services.
