# WAR-01 | Warranty claims after the arrival-damage window

Effective 2026-01-01. Version 1.

Warranty policy applies when a product worked after delivery but failed later, or when the customer reports defects outside the 30-day arrival-damage window. Warranty is not the same as arrival damage.

If the customer reports a problem on day 31 or later after delivery, the damaged-item automatic replacement path must not be used. Escalate under DAM-01 and include possible warranty context. A reviewer may approve repair, replacement, paid service, or denial depending on warranty terms.

Warranty requests may require serial number, purchase date, product condition, region, and proof of purchase. The agent should ask only for information needed for the next step and should not request unsafe troubleshooting.

Consumables, accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized repair, and normal wear may not be covered. The agent should not deny coverage automatically unless the policy data explicitly supports denial. Ambiguous warranty cases go to human review.

If the product is under a safety recall, escalate immediately and do not apply ordinary warranty troubleshooting.
